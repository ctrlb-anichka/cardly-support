# Cardly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Cardly is provided by Anna Yefimchuk. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information you enter

Cardly stores your business cards, contact details, selected images, and designs locally on your device. Cardly does not operate a server that receives this content.

Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple backup settings.

## Photos

Cardly uses Apple's photo picker to let you select images for logos and backgrounds. Selected images are used in your card designs and stored with your cards.

## Address search

If you use address search, your search queries are processed through Apple's MapKit services. Apple's handling of this information is governed by its privacy policy:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Cardly uses StoreKit transaction information to verify subscription access. Cardly does not receive your payment card details.

## Sharing and exporting

When you export or share a card, its content is included in the exported file. The apps, services, and recipients you choose may handle that information under their own privacy policies.

## Analytics and advertising

Cardly does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Apple may process diagnostic and usage information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

## Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

## Deleting your information

You can delete saved cards inside the app. Uninstalling the app may remove its local data. Exported files, shared copies, and device backups must be managed separately.

To request deletion of information you sent to support, contact us using the email below.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
